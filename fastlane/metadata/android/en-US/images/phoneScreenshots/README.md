# Phone screenshots — Google Play (en-US)

Place **2 to 8** files here: `1.png`, `2.png`, … `8.png` (order = display order in the store).

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | **1080 × 1920 px** (portrait, 9:16) — recommended |
| Accepted size | 320 to 3840 px per side; aspect ratio from 16:9 to 9:16 |
| Format | PNG or JPEG |
| File size | up to 8 MB each |
| Count | Play accepts **2 to 8**; the project docs require **at least 4** — prepare **6–8** |
| File names | `1.png` … `8.png` |

## Frames to capture (order matters)

| # | Screen | Caption EN |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home grid with large cards | `Only the videos you chose` |
| 2 | Collections with emoji | `Cartoons, learning, music — in folders` |
| 3 | Full-screen kid-friendly player | `Big buttons for little fingers` |
| 4 | "That's all for today" screen | `A daily screen-time limit` |
| 5 | Parent mode: PIN entry | `Parent mode, protected by a PIN` |
| 6 | Adding a video by link | `Add a video by link in 10 seconds` |
| 7 | Favourites | `Favourites, always at hand` |
| 8 | Viewing history | `See what your child watched` |

## Caption styling

- Headline on top, at most 2 lines, **Nunito ExtraBold**, 72–90 px on a 1080 px canvas.
- Subhead (optional) — 40–48 px, grey.
- Text colour — `#10214A` (`lightText`) on a light background, or `#F2F6FF` (`darkText`) on a coloured plate.
- The frame background is the real app interface. Do not add device frames.
- Do not cover important UI elements with the caption.

## Not allowed

- the YouTube, YouTube Kids or Google logos, or any other third-party trademark;
- real photographs or faces of children, or personal data;
- "working" buttons drawn on top of the screenshot, or calls to action such as "tap", "install";
- claims about features that do not exist ("ad-free", "video download");
- anything that is not in the real 1.0.0 build.
