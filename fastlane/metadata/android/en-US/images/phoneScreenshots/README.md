1. Only the videos you chose
2. Cartoons, learning and music — sorted into folders
3. Big buttons — easy for small fingers
4. A daily screen-time limit
5. Parent mode behind a PIN
6. Add a video by link in 10 seconds
7. Favourite videos always at hand
8. See what your child watched
